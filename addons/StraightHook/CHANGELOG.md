# Changelog

## 0.3.0
- **Where to fish, on the Fishing spell's tooltip.** Hover the Fishing ability (spellbook or action bar) to see:
  - the best zones where nothing gets away at your skill (bonuses included);
  - three "stretch" zones with their catch chance;
  - each zone's AH value per cast.

  Zone names are coloured by level against yours, like the world map. Skill-ups come only from catches, so any zone in the first list is a full-speed skill-up spot. `/hook guide off` hides it.
- **More honest values.** An item that has never sold counts only when it's listed in depth (10+ units), at its cheaper asking price. A lone overpriced listing no longer inflates a zone or a catch.

## 0.2.0
- **A fishing guide out of the box.** Every zone now shows its vanilla catch table, with percentages, AH values and the expected value per catch, before you've caught anything there. It's labelled as Classic data: Forever may differ.
- **Your catches take over.** After 10 catches at a spot, your own table leads, and each fish shows its Classic share next to yours. `≠` marks a real disagreement, and a Classic fish you have never caught is listed once the absence is significant (a fish missing from Forever, or a changed table).
- **Getaway warning.** The skill line says at what skill fish stop getting away at this spot, red if you're below it.
- Data: CMaNGOS classic-db (GPL-3.0), attribution in `reference/fishing/README.md`.

## 0.1.1
- **Bite probe fixed.** It measured "before your click" a second late, so it only saw the click and the loot. It now uses the moment you interact, and keeps the last 10 casts' full event timelines for inspection. Run `/hook probe reset`, then `/hook probe on`, and fish again.

## 0.1.0
- **Hear every bite.** While you fish, sound effects go to full and music, ambience and dialog go silent, and the game keeps playing sound when it is in the background (alt-tabbed). Everything is restored 8 s after you stop fishing, even after a crash or `/reload` mid-cast.
- **What this spot gives.** Every catch is logged per spot (zone + subzone). A small panel appears while you fish (drag to move) showing:
  - your Fishing skill;
  - casts, catches, fish that got away and bites you missed;
  - the catch table in %;
  - each fish's typical AH price at Horde (H) and Blackwater (B), and the expected value per catch;
  - fish per hour and gold per hour for the session.

  The same lines are added to the bobber's tooltip.
- **Bite probe** (`/hook probe on`, then `/hook probe report`): an experiment to find out whether the game sends addons any signal on the bite. If it does, the next version can play a loud ping on the bite itself.
- AH values need `Data/Prices.lua`, generated by the private market pipeline (`wowf-analysis export-addon`). Without it the panel works and leaves the values out.
